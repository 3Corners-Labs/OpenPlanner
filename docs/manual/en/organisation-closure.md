# Emergency closure of an organisation

The organisation's emergency closure closes every team of the organisation at once, for
instance during an outage that affects everyone. Open the screen through **Organisations →
organisation → Emergency closure**. Organisation admins and the superuser can use it.

## Teams

The table shows each team with its status: **Open**, or closed with the period of the closure and
the number of bookings on hold. A team's name opens that team's **Emergency closure** screen,
where you handle its bookings.

## Close every team in an emergency

Enter from when until when the teams are closed, and a notice for participants in Dutch and
English; you can also start from a scenario. Bookings in the closure are put on hold, not deleted,
and every participant gets an email with your explanation. Times are in the teams' time zone. A
team that is already closed stays as it is. What a closure does further is in *Emergency closure
of a team*.

## Lift everything

**Lift everything** lifts the closure of every team of the organisation that is closed or has a
closure planned, including a team you closed on its own. You choose whether the bookings on hold
are confirmed again.

## Alternative contact options

During a closure participants see how to reach the organisation otherwise. The link to
**Alternative contact options** opens the block that applies to the whole organisation.
