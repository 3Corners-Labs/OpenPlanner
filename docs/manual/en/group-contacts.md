# A team's contact options

The organisation records once how participants can reach it otherwise (the organisation's
**Alternative contact options** screen). Does a team have its own desk, another phone number or
other opening hours? Then fill in a block of its own for that team here. Open it through
**team → Closure → Manage alternative contact options**. A group admin, organisation admin or
superuser can change it.

## Which block applies

- If the team has a block of its own, that block applies.
- Otherwise the organisation's default applies.
- If there is neither, participants only see the text of the closure.

The preview at the top shows which block applies now. The form below holds only the team's own
block. Empty every row and save to remove it; the organisation's default applies again.

## What a closure takes over

When a closure starts, it takes a copy of the block that applies at that moment. If you change
the team's or the organisation's block afterwards, the running closure keeps showing the old
block. That way what participants were emailed matches what the page shows.

To put a changed block on the running closure anyway, tick **Take over the contact options that
apply to this team now** under **Update closure**.

## Contact options

The rows work as on the organisation's screen: at most six, each with a kind (phone, email,
website, chat or address) and its details, and optionally a name and opening hours in Dutch and
English. Links must start with `https://`.

## Public

Everything on this screen is public. Use only general numbers and addresses, never those of an
employee.

## What is recorded

Every change goes into the team's audit log, with the number of contact options per kind. The
numbers, addresses and texts themselves are not in the log.
