# My profile

This screen shows how you appear in the admin portal and lets you set the name a participant
sees for you in a call. Open it through **My rights** in the menu and then **My profile**, or
through your name at the top right. Everyone who can log in sees their own profile; no separate
permission is needed.

## What you see

- **Account name**: the name your organisation's login gave. You cannot change it here, and it
  is not what participants see.
- **Screen name**: the name a participant sees for you on the organisation's own call page, next
  to the organisation's word for staff ("Sanne · advisor"), and in the captions. A manager who
  may manage staff can change your screen name too (see the topic [Staff](staff.md)).

## Screen name

Fill in the name you want participants to see and choose **Save screen name**. An empty field
removes it; then participants see the organisation's word for staff alone, unless the
organisation chose to show the name of the account.

Choose a **first name or a made-up name**, not your full name, so that you cannot be found by
your account name. The name has 2 to 40 characters: letters of any script, digits, spaces, a
hyphen, an apostrophe and a full stop, with at least one letter or digit. Names are not checked
for uniqueness: choose one that does not look like a colleague's name or a function.

There is one screen name per person, whichever organisation's participants see it. It is
personal data: deleting your staff record deletes it, and the audit log says that you set or
cleared it, never what it is.
