# My sessions

This screen shows where you are logged in to the admin portal right now. Open it through **My
rights** in the menu and then **My sessions**. Everyone who can log in sees their own sessions;
no separate permission is needed.

## What you see

For each session, the moment of the login and the moment the session was last active. The
session you are looking with is marked **This session**. The screen shows no addresses, devices
or session numbers: OpenPlanner does not keep them.

One person can have several sessions at once, for example on a laptop and on a tablet. Tabs in
the same browser share one session. There are at most 5 per person (a setting of the
superuser). If you log in a sixth time, the session that has been idle longest lapses.

## End other sessions

The **End other sessions** button ends all sessions except this one. Use it when you left a
laptop open or do not recognise a session. The other sessions can no longer be used afterwards;
whoever opens them has to log in again. The action is in the audit log, with the number and
without session numbers.

## Ending someone else's session

Organisation admins and the superuser can end all sessions of a staff member. That is on the
staff member's page, under **Staff**. See the topic [Staff](staff.md).
