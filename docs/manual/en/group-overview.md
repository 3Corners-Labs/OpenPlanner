# A group's overview

The **Overview** tab is a group's first page. Open it through **Groups → group**. Everyone who
may see the group sees it; what you can click depends on your rights.

## Ready for bookings

At the top is what is still missing before the group can take appointments: staffing for the
coming weeks, message texts, a channel to send messages and a connection check without failures.
Each line says in words whether it is done, and links to the tab that fixes it when you may open
that tab. See *Groups* for what each line means.

## Settings at a glance

How long a call takes, the notice, how far ahead participants can book, whether the group works
with the contact center or with the own queue, the phone number, the Webex link and whether a
direct call is offered. **Change settings** opens the **Settings** tab (only if you may).

## Staffing and exceptions

The weekly blocks and the coming exception days. **Manage staffing** and **Manage exceptions**
open the **Planning** tab.

## What participants see

A preview of the free times in the next two weeks, as the appointment page would show them before
bookings and notice. You see at once whether the staffing is right.

## Your rights

At the bottom are the rights you hold for this group. Ask your organisation admin for one that is
missing.

## New group

You create a group on **Groups → New group** (organisation admins and the superuser). After
creating it you land on this tab; the checklist says what is still to do.
