# Holiday calendars

Holiday calendars list days on which participants cannot book. The superuser sets a calendar
up once; organisations and groups reuse it. Open the screen with **Holidays** in the menu. Only
the superuser maintains the calendars. Which calendars apply to an organisation is chosen on
the organisation's own **Holidays** screen, and a group can follow that choice, make its own
or open single days as usual.

## The calendars

The overview lists every calendar; a name leads to its dates.

<!-- field: name -->
### Name

The name of the calendar, for example "National holidays". Organisations and groups see it when
they choose.

<!-- field: slug -->
### Web address

A short name for the address bar (the field is called "Web address"), for example `national`. It cannot be changed later. You
change the name of the calendar afterwards, the web address stays.

With **Create calendar** you make an empty calendar and open it to add dates.

## One calendar

The page shows the dates of one year, by default the current year; the links under *Other
years* go to the year before and after. The header says by how many organisations and groups
the calendar is chosen. Years from 2000 to 2100 are possible.

### Add the Dutch national holidays

**Add the holidays of {year}** adds New Year's Day, Easter, King's Day, Liberation Day,
Ascension Day, Whitsun and Christmas for the year shown, with names in your language. Dates
that are already there stay as they are. Good Friday is not included: add it yourself where it
applies.

### Add a date

<!-- field: holiday_day -->
### Date

The day on which the calendar closes. The page then shows the year of that date.

Fill in the date and a name and choose **Add date**. Remove a date with **Remove** at its row.

### Change the name

The name can be changed. The calendar's web address stays the same, and organisations and groups
that use it follow the new name.

### Delete a calendar

**Delete {name}** is only offered while no organisation or group has chosen the calendar. All its
dates go with it.

## What a holiday does

On a holiday that applies to a group, the group is closed and participants cannot book. Two
things win over that: a staffing exception of the group's own on that day, and a holiday
that the group has opened as usual on its own **Holidays** screen.
